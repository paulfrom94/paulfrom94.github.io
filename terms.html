Terms of use

For personal use.

Northstar Personal Calendar is a private, single-user calendar display.

Service scope

The service reads the primary Google Calendar with read-only access and displays up to six events, including time, title, and location when available. It does not manage calendars or events.

Availability and accuracy

Displayed information may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable if Google access or the service is interrupted. Google Calendar is the authoritative source for Google’s event information.

Northstar Personal Calendar is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted service.

Access

Google authorization can be revoked through your Google Account permissions. The service owner can remove the local authorization. Further calendar reads require active authorization.

For support, contact the owner who provided this private app.
