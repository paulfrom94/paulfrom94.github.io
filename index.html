Overview Privacy Terms

Private calendar dashboard

Your calendar, at a glance.

Northstar Personal Calendar is a private, single-user dashboard that reads your primary Google Calendar and displays up to six upcoming events.

What it shows

For each event, the dashboard may display its time, title, and location. The card displays events stored in Google Calendar.

Access is read-only. Northstar can display calendar information, but it cannot create, change, or delete calendar events.

Account access

Google sign-in uses OpenID and email identity information, along with read-only Calendar access. You can revoke this app’s access in your Google Account permissions. The service owner can also remove the local authorization.

For support, contact the owner who provided this private app.
