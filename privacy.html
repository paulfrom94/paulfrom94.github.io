Northstar Personal Calendar
Overview Privacy Terms

Privacy

Your calendar data stays within the private service.

Northstar Personal Calendar is for one user. These pages describe how its Google authorization and calendar data are handled.

Information accessed

Google sign-in uses OpenID and email identity information. The app requests permission to view Calendar events. This integration reads the primary calendar and displays up to six current or upcoming events, including their date, time, title, and location when available. Responses may contain additional events that are filtered before display.

Storage and display

Authorization tokens persist in restrictive files on the service owner’s private Mac. Calendar events are handled by the private server and held in browser memory for display. Event data and tokens are excluded from browser startup and saved previews.

Use and control

This app does not use advertising, sell data, or use third-party analytics. The Calendar integration does not include AI processing or model training.

You can revoke access through your Google Account permissions. The service owner can remove the local authorization. Revoking access prevents further reads until authorization is granted again.

Public information pages

These information pages are hosted publicly by GitHub Pages. Google handles account sign-in and Calendar access. Each provider has its own privacy practices. The public pages contain no calendar data or tokens and have no access to the dashboard. They provide no private dashboard URL.

For support, contact the owner who provided this private app.
